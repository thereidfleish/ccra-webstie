Contact Us

For any computer requests, donations, or questions, please email ccra@cornell.edu.
You can also contact our executive board members directly.

If you are in the Ithaca, NY area, please feel free to stop by one of our meetings!

Meetings

To get involved in the club, the best way is to come to a meeting!

We are active throughout the academic year, not including breaks. Our current meeting times are below:

  • Sundays 1-3 pm in Gates Hall G11
    • Our weekend meetings are dedicated to working on repurposing computers for requests. They involve wiping hard drives, reinstalling Windows, replacing computer parts, troubleshooting issues, packing computers for donation, helping a community member fix their device, etc. All are welcome, no experience is necessary and we will teach you everything you need to know!

  • Weekday afternoon (TBA) in Gates Hall G09
    • Our weekday meetings are intended to be relaxed and open-ended, and give people more flexibility in terms of what they can do during the meeting. Members can repurpose computers, use our equipment and tools for their own hardware projects, or help out people who swing by.

The address for Gates Hall is 107 Hoy Rd, Ithaca, NY 14853. To get to our meeting rooms, come into Gates Hall through the main entrance and go down the stairs next to Gimme Coffee.

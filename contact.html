IMPORTANT: We are wrapping up our operations for the year. Our last meeting of the Fall 2025 semester will be on Sunday, December 7, 2025, 1-3 pm in Gates Hall G11. The address for Gates Hall is 107 Hoy Rd, Ithaca, NY 14853. Afterward, please continue to contact us for any requests, donations, or questions; however we might not be able to act on them until we reconvene for the spring semester in late January.

We look forward to welcoming our volunteers, partners, and community members to CCRA again in 2026!

Meetings

To get involved in the club, the best way is to come to a meeting!

We are active throughout the academic year, not including breaks. In Spring 2026, we plan to hold 1 weekend meeting and 1 weekday meeting per week. Both meetings will be held in the afternoon in Gates Hall.

On any given day, we will be wiping hard drives, reinstalling Windows, replacing computer parts, troubleshooting issues, packing computers for donation, helping a community member fix their device, and more! All are welcome, no experience is necessary and we will teach you everything you need to know!
